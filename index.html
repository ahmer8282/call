In Always-On mode, both mics stay live like a normal video call — tap the 🎤 to mute. In Push-to-Talk mode, hold the big button to transmit, release to listen. Video stays on the whole time either way.
Tip for UAE networks: this uses encrypted, standard HTTPS-style traffic (TURN over port 443) to help the call connect on mobile data and most WiFi. If a call won't connect on one network, try switching between WiFi and mobile data.
